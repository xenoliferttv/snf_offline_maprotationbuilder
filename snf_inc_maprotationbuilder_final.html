📘 Wardogs Server Rotation Guide

1. Where is my Server Config?

To apply your map rotations, you need to edit your server configuration file located on your BisectHosting Starbase panel. You can access this via the web file manager or an SFTP client.

Default Path: /home/container/Configs/ServerSettings.ini

2. How to Build Your Rotation

  • Drag & Drop: Grab whole regions, specific weather, or individual zones from the left library and drag them into your Active Rotation.
  • Multi-Select: Click and drag your mouse over multiple rows, or use Shift + Click to select chunks of maps at once.
  • Reordering: Grab the ⋮⋮ handle on any highlighted row to drag the entire block up or down.
  • Modifiers: Highlight your maps, check the Infantry or Hardcore boxes in the toolbar, and click "Apply to Selected".

3. Applying Changes to the Server

  1. Click the green Copy Config button at the top right of this tool.
  2. Open your ServerSettings.ini file in the Bisect panel.
  3. Scroll down until you find the [/Script/WDGame.WDServerMapRotationSettings] header.
  4. Highlight everything from RotationMode=... all the way down through the existing .RotationEntries=... lines.
  5. Paste your copied config to overwrite the old maps, save the file, and restart your Wardogs server!
